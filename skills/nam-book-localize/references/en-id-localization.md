# English and Indonesian localization

Use the project manifest and the approved terminology ledger inside the current localization report as the authority. These notes resolve common English–Indonesian choices without replacing project-specific terminology.

## Establish the register

Choose and record the reader relationship before translating. In Indonesian, keep `Anda`, `kamu`, impersonal constructions, and professional forms of address consistent with the approved voice. In English, keep the selected regional variant and level of formality consistent.

Prefer natural target-language syntax over source-language word order. Preserve technical precision, but do not leave avoidable English fragments in Indonesian prose. At first mention, an approved English technical term may follow the Indonesian term in parentheses when it helps readers search or communicate across languages.

## Terms and abbreviations

- Reuse an approved terminology-ledger entry whenever the meaning matches.
- Preserve generic drug names, scientific names, formulas, code, and identifiers unless an authoritative localized form is recorded.
- Expand an acronym independently in each locale at its first required use. Do not assume the localized expansion yields the same acronym.
- Record polysemous or context-dependent terms with domain and usage notes instead of forcing one global translation.
- Keep product names, organization names, examination names, and guideline titles in their official forms unless an official localized form exists.

## Numbers, units, and references

Locale formatting may change separators, date order, or typographic spacing; it must not change numeric value, sign, exponent, dose, range, unit, equation, table alignment, or statistical meaning. Recheck every conversion-like edit independently. Preserve citation IDs and source titles; translate a bibliographic title only when an official translated title is available and the citation style calls for it.

## Teaching and assessment objects

Localize the function of a callout, not merely its label. Re-test mnemonics, analogies, humor, and cultural examples; replace them only when the same objective and evidence remain intact, and document the adaptation.

For assessments, check that grammar does not reveal the key, option lengths remain balanced, negatives remain conspicuous, and every distractor expresses the same misconception after translation. A translated item can be grammatically accurate yet invalid.

## Visuals and accessibility

Translate captions, alt text, legends, and figure-label manifests by stable `figure_id`. Preserve reading order and text alternatives. Create a localized rendering request for text embedded in artwork; keep the source asset unchanged.
