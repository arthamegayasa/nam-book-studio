# Drafting by book mode

Apply the active base mode from the project manifest. A project may override individual features, but the override belongs in the mode profile rather than being improvised chapter by chapter.

## Friendly explainer

Write for an intelligent reader who lacks specialist prerequisites.

- Establish why the concept matters before expanding terminology.
- Introduce one conceptual burden at a time and define jargon at first use.
- Move from a concrete situation to the general model, then test the model with a new example.
- Use a warm, adult tone. Plain language must remain precise rather than patronizing.
- Place optional technical depth after the usable explanation.
- End a substantial section with a brief retrieval opportunity or application, not another paraphrased summary.

Possible components include a hook, Big Idea, Why It Matters, explanation, worked example, misconception, visual, quick check, and concise recap. Use only components that serve the objective. Do not imitate a named book series, its recurring language, or its visual trade dress.

## Textbook

Write a systematic account that can support a course or sustained independent study.

- Make prerequisites and learning objectives visible.
- Establish definitions and notation before relying on them.
- Organize from foundations through mechanism, evidence, application, and limits.
- Keep terminology and granularity consistent across chapters.
- Use cases, figures, and tables as evidence-bearing instructional objects rather than decoration.
- Distinguish consensus, emerging evidence, controversy, and author interpretation.
- Close with a synthesis tied to objectives, references, and self-assessment links.

## Exam preparation

Teach a compact mental model before compressing it into high-yield review.

- Follow the verified exam blueprint and edition recorded in the project.
- Separate durable understanding from facts that are primarily tested conventions.
- Surface discriminating features, near-neighbor contrasts, common reasoning errors, and time-saving decision paths.
- Mark any difference between the likely exam answer and jurisdiction-specific current practice.
- Reserve Exam Trap and High-Yield labels for genuinely discriminating points.
- Keep the answer and rationale out of the immediate question context unless the delivery format is formative.

Possible components include Must Know, mechanism, comparison table, algorithm, Clinical Pearl, Exam Trap, memory aid, worked case, rapid review, and practice-set link. Item writing belongs to `$nam-book-assess`.

## Shared drafting checks

- Every section advances an objective.
- Every factual claim is supported at the risk level required by the project.
- Examples do not quietly broaden a rule beyond its evidence.
- Analogies identify their boundary.
- Headings describe content rather than merely naming a format.
- Recaps compress; they do not introduce new claims.
